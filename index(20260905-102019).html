This app estimates formula supply. It does not diagnose feeding problems or select a medically appropriate formula. Follow the current product label and clinician guidance. Powdered formula is not sterile. Formula does not need to be warmed. If warmed, put a few drops of formula on your wrist to test the temperature. It should feel warm, not hot.
